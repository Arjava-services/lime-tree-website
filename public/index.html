Frequently Asked Questions

Can I order products from all 3 brands together? +
Yes! That is the core beauty of LimeTree. You can add clothing from Riyaa Trends, candles from Glow Home, and ear studs from Earkart into one single cart and receive them in one packaged shipment.
Are the ear studs suitable for newly pierced or sensitive ears? +
All Earkart ear studs are 100% hypoallergenic, nickel-free, and lead-free. We use solid 925 sterling silver with rhodium coating, 18k gold vermeil, and medical-grade titanium cores.
How long do Glow Home candles burn? +
Our 8 oz to 9 oz natural soy candles have a clean, slow burn of 50 to 60 hours. We recommend trimming the crackling wood wick to 1/4 inch before each burn.
