The Artisan Candle Care Ritual

The Memory Burn:

On your first lighting, allow wax to melt across the entire circumference of the jar (2–3 hours) to prevent candle tunneling.

Trim the Wick:

Always trim the braided cotton wick to 1/4 inch (6mm) before relighting to preserve a clean, soot-free flame.

Safe Placement:

Burn within sight on heat-resistant surfaces. Discontinue burning when 1/2 inch of wax remains to protect the glass vessel.
