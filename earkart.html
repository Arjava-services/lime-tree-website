Precious Metal Care & Longevity

The Last Step Rule:

Put your jewellery on after applying perfume, hairspray, and lotions to safeguard the 18K vermeil luster.

Water & Workout Care:

Remove prior to swimming in chlorinated pools or rigorous workouts to prevent accidental chemical discoloration.

Anti-Tarnish Storage:

Store individual pieces in the included Limetree velvet pouch with anti-tarnish tab when not in rotation.
